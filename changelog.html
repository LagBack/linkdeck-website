v1.0.0
Initial release

The first version of LinkDeck.

A visual home for browser bookmarks: organized into cards, made searchable, and styled with distinct themes. The first release lays the foundation for a personal web desk that can keep growing.

  • AddedVisual bookmark cards with page previews and key details at a glance.
  • AddedSearch across bookmark titles, URLs, tags, and notes.
  • AddedCategories, tags, notes, favorites, and grid/list browsing.
  • AddedMultiple visual themes, including LinkDeck Original, Windows 98 / XP, SceneCore, GXSC, and Pixel Night.
  • AddedOptional local page screenshot capture and optional online thumbnail previews.
  • AddedOptional installed-extension shortcuts, theme-first onboarding, and import/export for notes.
  • PrivacyBookmark organization and notes are stored locally; online previews are optional and send selected URLs to thum.io when enabled.
